## Summary

Construction tasks perform additional uncapped subset searches after the bounded exact-minimality search. The second pass can dominate generation time and defeat the first search's call budget.

This was reproduced from owner `main` at `1fdc9db`.

## Evidence

[`minimal_subset_exact`](https://github.com/ieddeveci/ArgGYM/blob/1fdc9db03ddcc415abd82bef0f230ab31b8a6892/ArgGYM_v2/core/invariants.py#L215-L255) has a call budget. Immediately afterward, `counter_argument` calls [`assert_irredundant`](https://github.com/ieddeveci/ArgGYM/blob/1fdc9db03ddcc415abd82bef0f230ab31b8a6892/ArgGYM_v2/tasks/counter_argument.py#L286-L294), which enumerates every proper subset without a budget ([`invariants.py`](https://github.com/ieddeveci/ArgGYM/blob/1fdc9db03ddcc415abd82bef0f230ab31b8a6892/ArgGYM_v2/core/invariants.py#L38-L44)). `preference_construction` likewise performs extra singleton and pair searches after its bounded exact search ([`preference_construction.py`](https://github.com/ieddeveci/ArgGYM/blob/1fdc9db03ddcc415abd82bef0f230ab31b8a6892/ArgGYM_v2/tasks/preference_construction.py#L236-L261)).

Observed official cells on the audit machine included:

```text
counter_argument, L12 / seed 1 / weakest-link: >30 s
counter_argument, L15 / seed 1 / last-link:    >30 s
```

Other level-12 cells took 1.87–3.45 seconds. A 20-cell `preference_construction` batch was stopped after two minutes while executing post-search checks. Exact timings are environment-specific; interrupted stacks were inside `assert_irredundant` or post-minimality `holds` calls.

## Impact

The advertised budget does not bound end-to-end generation work. A single cell can stall an export, and runtime varies sharply across seeds with the same nominal level.

## Suggested fix

Have the bounded search return the minimality evidence callers need and remove the duplicate exhaustive pass. Carry one explicit call or time budget through every fallback, and record budget exhaustion in item or export metadata.
