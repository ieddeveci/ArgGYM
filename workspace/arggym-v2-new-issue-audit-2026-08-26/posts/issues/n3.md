## Summary

The exporter silently skips requested grid cells when generation returns `None`. It writes a smaller, differently weighted taskset without failing or recording which cells are missing.

This was reproduced from owner `main` at `1fdc9db`.

## Evidence

[`export_task`](https://github.com/ieddeveci/ArgGYM/blob/1fdc9db03ddcc415abd82bef0f230ab31b8a6892/ArgGYM_v2/core/export.py#L86-L96) continues past any generator result of `None`.

On the default five-level, two-ordering, two-seed grid, both `status_query` cells at level 12 with last-link ordering and seeds 0 and 1 exhausted the task's 24 attempts ([`status_query.py`](https://github.com/ieddeveci/ArgGYM/blob/1fdc9db03ddcc415abd82bef0f230ab31b8a6892/ArgGYM_v2/tasks/status_query.py#L338-L344)). Export then reported success with only 18 of the requested 20 rows:

```text
wrote 18 items (18 valid) -> .../status_query.jsonl
```

The manifest says `n_items: 18` and repeats all five requested levels and both orderings, but it has no missing-cell field. A manual grid for `perturbation` also lost `(level=15, ordering=last_link_elitist, seed=0)`.

## Impact

Missing cells change the relative weight of tasks, levels, and orderings in aggregate scores. Because export exits successfully and the manifest does not identify the omissions, downstream users cannot distinguish a complete taskset from a partially generated one.

## Suggested fix

Require every requested `(level, ordering, seed)` cell by default. If partial export is needed, put it behind an explicit `--allow-missing` option and record the exact missing tuples and generation failures in the manifest.
