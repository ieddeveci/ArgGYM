## Summary

`semantics_query` rebuilds a verifier and recomputes preferred or stable extensions for every candidate-and-semantics pair. High-level official cells can spend tens of seconds repeating the same extension enumeration.

This was reproduced from owner `main` at `1fdc9db`.

## Evidence

[`status_under`](https://github.com/ieddeveci/ArgGYM/blob/1fdc9db03ddcc415abd82bef0f230ab31b8a6892/ArgGYM_v2/tasks/semantics_query.py#L74-L110) constructs a fresh verifier on every call and enumerates the requested semantics' extensions. [`build`](https://github.com/ieddeveci/ArgGYM/blob/1fdc9db03ddcc415abd82bef0f230ab31b8a6892/ArgGYM_v2/tasks/semantics_query.py#L297-L303) calls it for each candidate-and-semantics pair, and generation may retry the entire build 24 times ([`semantics_query.py`](https://github.com/ieddeveci/ArgGYM/blob/1fdc9db03ddcc415abd82bef0f230ab31b8a6892/ArgGYM_v2/tasks/semantics_query.py#L416-L422)).

On the audit machine:

```text
level 12 / seed 0 / last-link: 3.62 s
level 15 / seed 0 / last-link: exceeded a 30 s cap
```

The interrupted level-15 stack was inside PyArg's preferred-extension recursion. These absolute times are environment-specific; the repeated calls are visible directly in the source.

## Impact

Export cost grows with both extension enumeration and the number of query pairs, even though all pairs share one theory. Slow cells also compound the silent missing-cell behavior when generation retries or fails.

## Suggested fix

Construct one verifier per generated theory. Enumerate each requested semantics once, cache the relevant conclusion sets, and answer all candidate queries from those cached results.
