does, does strict success on the dev split rise above the base model's?

## How the settings were reached

Smoke runs before this one (wandb `dvve93cv`, `cwwd2pge`, `omlye2ad`,
`8bvw8add`) fixed two settings.

| Training sampling | Truncated at 40,960 | Groups with no signal |
|---|---|---|
| T 1.0, top_p 1.0, top_k off | 42-52% (levels 1-4) | 0.62-0.75 |
| + top_p 0.95, top_k 20 | 42-58% (levels 1-8) | 0.38-0.75 |
| + presence_penalty 1.5 | 20-28% (levels 1-8) | 0.25-0.50 |

Without the card's penalty the model loops until the cap. The penalty and the
truncated sampling make vLLM's distribution differ from the one the trainer
scores; the per-token log-prob gap rose from 0.011 to 0.08, and the importance
weights absorb it (minimum ratio about 0.1, cap 2).

## Discussion

No gain yet, and 20 steps is little: 160 prompts, about half of whose groups
carried no gradient. Train success rose from 0.29 to 0.35 across the first four
windows, but the curriculum changes which prompts are drawn, so that is not
evidence of learning.

The curriculum lowered wasted groups less than expected. The pass rate of a
(task, level) cell predicts a single prompt poorly: within one cell, prompts
went 8/8 and 0/8 (preference_construction L8 in steps 7 and 8; L4 at 8/8 and L5
at 0/8 with the same 0.5 prior). The variance that matters is between prompts,
which a cell-level sampler cannot see. Two steps of lag between an outcome and
its effect on sampling add to it.

Next, decided at the step-40 dev eval:

- **If dev rises**, keep running and confirm the selected checkpoint on the
  lite eval against `results/hf-qwen3.5-4b__xml_tags__cot`.
- **If dev is flat**, screen prompts instead of cells: sample each pool prompt
  4 times with the current model on all 3 GPUs and train only on prompts that
  came out mixed. Raise the LR to 2-3e-5, since gradient norms are small.
